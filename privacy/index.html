Jass Stübli – Privacy

November 2026

Deutsch

Jass Stübli does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics and no third-party service.

Settings, saved games and statistics stay on your iPhone. If you delete the app, they are gone.

When you play together, the app connects to the iPhones near you over the local network. The connection is encrypted and goes through no server. Only what the table needs is exchanged: the names at the table and the moves.
